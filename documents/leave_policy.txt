HR LEAVE POLICY — TRAINING EXAMPLE
Annual leave: Eligible full-time employees receive 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year.
Request process: Submit a leave request to your manager at least 5 working days before planned leave, where practical.
Approval: Leave is not approved until the manager confirms it in the leave system.
Carry-over: Unused leave rules depend on the employment agreement and local policy. Contact HR if unsure.
